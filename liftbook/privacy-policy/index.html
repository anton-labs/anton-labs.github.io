Liftbook

Privacy Policy for a local-first workout tracker.

Effective date: April 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Liftbook ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Liftbook mobile application.

1. Summary

Liftbook is a workout tracking application that stores and processes workout information primarily on your device. The current version of Liftbook does not require account registration and does not sell your personal information.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the app, Liftbook may process the following information that you enter or generate:

  • Workout history
  • Exercise names, categories, and muscle group selections
  • Sets, repetitions, weight, rest times, and workout notes
  • Personal records
  • Workout templates
  • Workout dates, durations, and calculated totals such as training volume
  • App preferences such as your name, weight unit, and distance unit

Some of this information may be considered health- or fitness-related information under applicable law.

3. How We Use Information

Liftbook uses information only to operate and improve core app features, including:

  • Saving and displaying your workouts
  • Restoring active workout progress
  • Showing workout history and exercise details
  • Calculating statistics, trends, achievements, and personal records
  • Saving your preferences
  • Importing and exporting workout data when you request those actions

The current version of Liftbook performs these functions locally on your device.

4. Where Information Is Stored

Liftbook currently stores information locally on your device using:

  • IndexedDB for workouts, exercises, templates, and personal records
  • localStorage for active workout state and app preferences

The current version of Liftbook does not require a remote user account or cloud sync service.

5. Import and Export

Liftbook allows you to:

  • Export your workout data to a JSON file on your device
  • Import workout data from a JSON file that you select

Exported files may contain your workout history, exercises, templates, and personal records. You are responsible for the security, storage, and sharing of files after you export them.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

The current version of Liftbook does not sell your personal information.

Based on the current app code, Liftbook does not include built-in advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social-sharing SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs that automatically send your workout data to third parties.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.

7. Internet Access

The Android app currently declares internet permission. However, based on the current version of the app, Liftbook does not currently transmit your workout or profile data to our servers or to third-party advertising or analytics services.

If a future version adds cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, payment processing, or customer support tools that process personal information, this Privacy Policy should be updated before that version is released.

8. Backups

The current Android configuration disables Android Auto Backup in the app manifest.

Please note that any files you export manually are under your control and may be stored wherever you choose to save them.

9. Logging

Liftbook may write limited diagnostic or error information to local logs or the local browser or WebView console during operation. The current version does not include a remote logging or crash-reporting service.

10. Data Retention

Your data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the app, uninstall it, or overwrite it through an import action.

If you export data to files, those files remain wherever you save them until you delete them.

11. Security

Liftbook is designed to minimize unnecessary transmission of personal information by storing data locally on your device. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure. You are responsible for securing access to your device and for protecting any exported files.

12. Children

Liftbook is not directed to children under the age at which they can legally consent to data processing in their jurisdiction.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at info.antonlabs@gmail.com.

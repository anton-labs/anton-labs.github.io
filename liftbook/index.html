Liftbook

Workout tracking without distractions.

Liftbook is a simple workout tracker built for people who want to log training without distractions. Record exercises, sets, reps, weights, personal records, and workout notes in a fast, local-first app designed to work offline. Track your progress over time, review workout history, build reusable templates, and keep your training data on your own device.
